How it was judged

Case-insensitive visible-text matching for organization name and address; phone matching ignores punctuation. Results describe this cohort on this date, not a guarantee that any live website remains correct.

Decision and gates

Scrapling is the best experimental candidate. It is not a production adoption: discovery remains disabled and scheduled work is unchanged.

Required before adoption
Explicit source-policy approval; a policy-bound plain-Fetcher adapter; then a small human-reviewed canary through the existing evidence and review workflow.
